Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding appointments, preparation, tests, and chamber visits.

What documents or reports should I bring for my first consultation?

Please bring all recent diagnostic reports, laboratory test results, previous prescriptions, imaging discs (X-ray, MRI, CT, Echo), and a list of medications you are currently taking.

How can I book an appointment or serial for chamber visits?

You can use the online appointment booking form on this website, call the direct hotline at +880 1609-977377, or message via WhatsApp for instant appointment confirmation.

Is online / telehealth video consultation available?

Yes, Dr. Nakamura offers scheduled telehealth consultations for follow-up patients and overseas or remote consultations via secure video link.

What are the visiting hours and chamber locations?

Regular chamber hours are Saturday to Thursday from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM at Specialist Diagnostic Center, Los Angeles, CA. Friday is reserved for emergency surgeries and academic rounds.
