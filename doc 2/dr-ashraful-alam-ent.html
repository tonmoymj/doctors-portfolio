About

Fixing the small things that affect everything.

Dr. Ashraful Alam earned his MBBS from Rajshahi Medical College and his MS in Otolaryngology (ENT) from Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University. He now leads the ENT department at Rajshahi ENT & Hearing Center.

He treats hearing, breathing, and voice problems as connected systems — a blocked nose, a ringing ear, and a hoarse voice often share one root cause, and he digs until he finds it.
