About

Slow, careful, and genuinely curious.

Dr. Mehjabin Chowdhury completed her MBBS at Rajshahi Medical College and her MD in Psychiatry at the National Institute of Mental Health, Dhaka. She spent two years training in cognitive behavioral therapy alongside medication management.

Her sessions run longer than most — she believes accurate diagnosis takes time, and that people deserve to feel heard before anything is prescribed.
