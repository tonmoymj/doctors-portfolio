ABOUT

Precision-focused, recovery-obsessed.

Dr. Shahriar Kabir earned his MS in Orthopedic Surgery from Chittagong Medical College and trained further in joint replacement surgery in South Korea. He now leads Orthopedic Services at Rajshahi Bone & Joint Hospital.

He treats every fracture and joint problem as an engineering puzzle — load, alignment, and recovery time all matter. Patients get a clear map of what surgery (or non-surgical treatment) will realistically achieve.
