About

A dentist who explains every step first.

Dr. Imran Hossain completed his BDS at Dhaka Dental College and his advanced training in Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry in Bangkok. He founded BrightSmile Dental Care to make routine and complex dental work equally stress-free.

He walks every patient through the dental chart before starting, so there's never a surprise mid-procedure — just a clear plan, tooth by tooth.
