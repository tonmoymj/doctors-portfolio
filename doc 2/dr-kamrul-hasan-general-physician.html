About

Trusted care, close to home.

Dr. Kamrul Hasan completed his MBBS at Rajshahi Medical College and holds an MRCGP from the Royal College of General Practitioners, UK. He has run Rajshahi Family Clinic since 2010, treating patients across every stage of life.

Many of his patients have been with him for over a decade — he's treated grandparents, their children, and now their grandchildren, building the kind of continuity that catches problems early.
