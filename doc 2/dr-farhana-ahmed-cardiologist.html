About

A cardiologist who explains before she treats.

Dr. Farhana Ahmed completed her MBBS at Rajshahi Medical College, followed by an MD in Cardiology and a fellowship in Interventional Cardiology in Singapore. She currently leads the Coronary Care Unit at Rajshahi Heart Institute.

Her approach centers on plain-language conversations — patients leave every visit understanding exactly what's happening in their heart and why each next step matters. She has a particular interest in preventive cardiology for patients with a family history of early heart disease.
