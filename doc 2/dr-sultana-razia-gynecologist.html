About

A gynecologist who listens before she examines.

Dr. Sultana Razia earned her MBBS from Rajshahi Medical College and her FCPS in Obstetrics & Gynecology from the Bangladesh College of Physicians and Surgeons. She has delivered over 3,000 babies and guided countless women through every stage of reproductive health.

Her consultations move at the patient's pace — every question answered, every concern taken seriously, whether it's a first prenatal visit or a menopause consultation.
