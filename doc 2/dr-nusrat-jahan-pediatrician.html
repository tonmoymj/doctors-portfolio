About

A pediatrician who gets down to their level.

Dr. Nusrat Jahan completed her MBBS at Rajshahi Medical College and her DCH (Diploma in Child Health) at Dhaka Shishu Hospital. She has spent the last decade focused entirely on children — from premature newborns to teenagers.

She believes the best pediatric care happens when kids feel safe enough to relax. Her clinic room has a growth chart wall every child gets to mark on their own visit.
