About

A neurologist who traces symptoms to their source.

Dr. Rakibul Islam earned his MD in Neurology from Dhaka Medical College and completed advanced training in epilepsy and stroke management at Christian Medical College, Vellore. He now heads the Neurology division at Rajshahi Neuro Care Center.

He believes most neurological conditions are manageable once properly understood — his consultations focus on clear diagnosis, honest prognosis, and a treatment path patients can actually follow at home.
